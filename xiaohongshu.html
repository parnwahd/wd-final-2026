Xiaohongshu

Xiaohongshu, also known as RedNote, was founded in 2013 by Miranda Qu. Qu's initial idea for Xiaohongshu was to give Chinese travelers insight into shopping abroad, allowing users to share experiences and product reviews on the platform. With a large community built around user-generated content (UGC), Xiaohongshu developed into a lifestyle-focused platform where people share trends and experiences in fashion, beauty, travel, and other lifestyle topics through photos, text, videos, and live streaming. Xiaohongshu’s core demographic is young urban consumers, with women making up over 70% of users.

Unlike traditional e-commerce platforms that focus on direct selling, Xiaohongshu builds trust through visually appealing and aesthetic user-generated content (UGC). Its social nature encourages consumers to discover products through attractive lifestyle content and see how other users use and recommend them, creating a more engaging and pleasing product discovery experience.

Recognizing these consumer patterns, Xiaohongshu enabled brands to set up storefronts in-app and partner with influencers with large audiences to promote their brands. This approach not only increases brand visibility but also strengthens credibility, as consumers can discover products through trusted content and purchase them directly within the platform.

By connecting user-generated content, social influence, and in-app shopping, Xiaohongshu turned social interaction, storytelling, and lifestyle sharing into an important part of the e-commerce experience.
